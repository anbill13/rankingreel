**Short version:** a Shorts-only "ranking compilation" channel that exploded in 2025 and is now coasting. It gets about 90K views a day, but almost none of those views turn into subscribers.

## 1. Growth trajectory: plateauing

- **Current size:** 158K subscribers, 368M total views and 255 videos. The channel was created in Feb 2025.
- **Last 30 days:** about +2K subscribers (YouTube rounds to the nearest 1K, so treat that as approximate) and +2.8M views from 8 uploads.
- **Compared with its own history:** 90–95K views a day is roughly 6x below its lifetime average of about 585K a day.
- **Subscriber conversion:** about 0.04% of lifetime views became subscribers. That is very weak. The audience is passive Shorts-feed traffic, not a community.
- **Declining ceiling:** 2025 had four Shorts above 30M views. In 2026 the best is 18.7M (Feb). Uploads from Jun–Sep 2026 mostly landed between 0.4M and 4M, and the most recent ones are well under 1M.

## 5. What you can do differently

1. **Own the "ranking" format with a recognizable angle.** The competitor's titles are generic and often identical. Specific hooks and numbers in titles ("Top 5 … that nobody saw coming") give you something they don't.
2. **Fix subscriber conversion.** A recurring host or voice, a consistent series identity and a reason to follow would be worth more than raw views. At 0.04%, a channel with a fraction of their views could still out-grow them in subscribers.
3. **Add original value to the clips.** The model depends entirely on third-party footage under a "fair use" disclaimer. That is a monetization and reused-content risk for them. Commentary, your own reactions or permissioned clips protect you.
4. **Fill the long-form gap.** They have none, so extended "Top 20" compilations are open ground.
5. **Test broadly, then double down.** Their winners were broad themes, and their winners re-surfaced for weeks. Test many topics quickly, but don't write off a video before day 7, and keep checking for later waves.
6. **Steer clear of declining topics** such as wingsuit and base jumping. Aim at rising ones (meme-adjacent, "impressive" skill clips).

## Caveats

- Daily stats are available only for the windows pulled, and 3 of the 4 deep dives stop early. Later behavior for Mom Into Me and Impressive Videos is inferred from final totals.
- The empty long-form result could also be a data gap, not proof that the channel has no long-form videos.
